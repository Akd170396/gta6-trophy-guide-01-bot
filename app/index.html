    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters first while the missable windows are open, then strangers, activities and crime, collectibles and exploration, money and property, and finally 100% completion. Stages check off as you earn their trophies.

    Missable trophies

    Flagged from the official descriptions alone: each of these is tied to a one-time story beat, a choice, a named character’s request or a single strange event. The flag basis is stated in every guide. Keep rotating manual saves before story missions and you can always reload.

    Next up

    Your next trophies in roadmap order — earliest unfinished stage first, missables before anything else in their stage. Tick one off and the list refills.

    Save & settings

    Your progress

    Progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) as you tick trophies, move counters and write notes. Nothing leaves your machine — this guide makes no network requests.

    Export & import

    Export your checklist to a JSON file, and import it back on any machine. Import replaces the current progress after a confirmation.

    Spoiler setting

    Spoiler-free mode hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them, so the checklist never spoils a chapter you have not reached.

    Reset

    Clear every tick, counter and note and start the hunt again. Export first if you want to keep a copy.
